About AI Tools Guide

AI Tools Guide is an independent blog focused on one thing: helping content creators, freelancers and small businesses choose the right AI tools.

What we do

Every week, new AI tools launch promising to save you time or make you money. Most reviews online are either sponsored or written by people who never actually used the tool. We do it differently:

  • We test tools ourselves before writing about them.
  • We compare honestly - including free limits, pricing traps and real weaknesses.
  • We write for creators - practical guides, not technical papers.

Our focus areas

  • AI video generation and cartoon/animation workflows
  • AI voiceovers, including Urdu and Hindi voices
  • AI image generation for thumbnails and content
  • AI writing assistants and prompt techniques
  • Realistic ways to earn money using AI tools

How we make money

This site is supported by advertising (such as Google AdSense) and occasionally affiliate links. When you click an affiliate link and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Our reviews and rankings are never sold - a tool cannot pay for a better rating.
