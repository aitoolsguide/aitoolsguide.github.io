How AI is used in recruiting

Sourcing

AI sourcing tools scan hundreds of millions of profiles, rank candidates by fit, and rediscover past applicants already in your database. SeekOut and Eightfold AI are the strongest examples.

Resume screening

AI screening parses resumes in seconds and scores them against the job description. Modern systems match on skills rather than keywords, catching strong candidates whose resumes use different wording.

Interview scheduling

Scheduling is one of hiring's biggest time sinks. Conversational AI like Paradox's Olivia chats with candidates over text, asks screening questions, and books slots into calendars — 24/7, in multiple languages.

Video interviews and assessments

Candidates record answers on their own schedule, and AI analyzes responses against job-relevant competencies to shortlist who moves forward. HireVue pioneered this category.

Bias considerations

The biggest risk is bias baked into training data or proxy variables. Reputable vendors publish bias audits, hide demographic data from screeners, and emphasize skills-based matching. Treat AI as decision support — humans make the final call — and check local rules: New York City's Local Law 144 requires independent bias audits.

1. Eightfold AI

The talent-intelligence platform for large organizations. Eightfold's deep-learning models analyze resumes, career sites, and your applicant database to match people to roles on skills and potential, not job titles — also powering AI career sites and internal mobility. Standout feature: skills-based matching that considers a candidate's full career trajectory. Limitation: custom enterprise pricing (five to six figures annually) — overkill under a few hundred employees.

2. Paradox (Olivia)

Paradox's conversational assistant Olivia is built for high-volume hourly and frontline hiring — retail, hospitality, warehouses, healthcare. Candidates apply through a mobile chat that screens them, answers questions about pay and shifts, and schedules interviews automatically. Standout feature: candidates go from job ad to scheduled interview in minutes, entirely over text. Limitation: designed for volume hiring; it feels thin for specialized white-collar roles, and pricing is custom enterprise.

3. HireVue

The leader in AI video interviewing and assessments. Candidates record structured video responses and complete game-based or technical assessments, scored against validated job competencies. HireVue dropped facial-analysis scoring years ago over bias concerns. Best for mid-to-large companies standardizing early-stage screening. Standout feature: structured video interviews plus validated assessments in one workflow. Limitation: custom enterprise pricing (mid-five-figures annually minimum), and some candidates dislike one-way video interviews.

4. Workable AI

An all-in-one ATS with AI through the workflow: an AI Recruiter sourcing from 400M+ profiles, AI resume screening, and an AI job-description writer. The easiest on-ramp to AI recruiting for teams without a dedicated HR tech person. Standout feature: the AI Recruiter sourcing add-on, finding passive candidates without leaving the ATS. Limitation: sourcing costs extra on top of base plans, and matching is good but not as deep as dedicated talent-intelligence platforms. Starts around $149/mo.

5. Zoho Recruit

AI recruiting for tight budgets. Zia, the built-in AI assistant, parses resumes, matches candidates to jobs, predicts availability, and flags data issues — with natural integration into the Zoho suite. Standout feature: genuinely capable AI from around $25/user/mo, with a free plan for a single recruiter. Limitation: the interface feels dated next to Workable or Greenhouse, and Zia's matching is solid but less sophisticated than Eightfold's or SeekOut's. If price matters most, this is the value pick.

6. Greenhouse

The structured-hiring ATS for companies that take process seriously — scorecards, interview kits, DEI-friendly workflows — with AI-assisted interview question generation and automated candidate summaries. Best for teams wanting AI on top of a disciplined hiring process. Standout feature: the structured methodology — every candidate evaluated against the same criteria, with AI summaries cutting reading time. Limitation: built-in AI is lighter than competitors' (much comes via marketplace integrations); pricing is custom, typically low thousands per year for small teams.

7. SeekOut

The AI sourcing engine for hard-to-fill roles. SeekOut indexes over 800 million profiles, applies AI matching with explainable "why this candidate" reasons, and offers diversity filters plus talent-insight analytics. Best for sourcing specialists hunting scarce skills. Standout feature: explainable matching plus talent analytics — you see not just who fits, but why. Limitation: a sourcing tool, not an ATS (pair it with Greenhouse or Workable); team licenses typically start in the low five figures per year.

Our verdict

  • Best overall talent platform: Eightfold AI — unmatched skills-based matching and internal mobility for large enterprises.
  • Best for high-volume hiring: Paradox (Olivia) — fastest path from job ad to scheduled interview for hourly and frontline roles.
  • Best for small teams and startups: Zoho Recruit — real AI features at a fraction of the cost, with a usable free plan. Workable AI wins if you can stretch to ~$149/mo and want better sourcing.
  • Best for specialized sourcing: SeekOut — pair it with Greenhouse for a best-of-breed stack.
  • Best for consistent screening: HireVue — structured interviews and validated assessments beat gut-feel review, as long as candidates accept the format.

One caution: AI accelerates your funnel — and your mistakes. Audit outcomes by demographic group, keep humans in the final decision, and check local bias-audit rules before deploying automated screening.

FAQ

Will AI replace recruiters?

No — it replaces recruiter busywork. Screening, scheduling, and sourcing get automated; relationship-building, persuasion, and final judgment stay human.

Is AI resume screening biased?

It can be, if trained on biased historical data or proxy signals like name or employment gaps. Modern tools counter this with skills-based matching, demographic blinding, and published bias audits. Ask any vendor for their audit and keep a human reviewing borderline decisions.

What is the best free AI recruitment tool?

Zoho Recruit's free plan for a single recruiter — the only free option here with AI features (resume parsing and Zia matching).

Can AI write job descriptions?

Yes — Workable AI, Zoho Recruit, and Greenhouse all generate them from a job title and a few inputs. Review for inflated requirements (a classic AI habit) and cut anything non-essential.

How much does AI recruiting software cost?

Widely: Zoho Recruit starts around $25/user/mo, Workable around $149/mo, and enterprise platforms use custom pricing — typically low thousands to six figures per year depending on headcount and modules.
