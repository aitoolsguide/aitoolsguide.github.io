Best AI Interior Design Tools in 2026 (Redesign Any Room)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read · Category: Design

Upload a photo of your living room and watch it reimagined in modern, boho, or minimalist style in thirty seconds. AI interior design tools make this real: they restyle photos, build 3D floor plans, and even show furniture in your actual space through AR. Whether you're redecorating on a budget or staging a home to sell, here's how the six best tools compare.

Quick Verdict

Short version: Planner 5D as the best all-rounder with real 3D planning, RoomGPT for instant free room restyling, REimagineHome for photorealistic style renders, Homestyler for detailed 3D floor plans, DecorMatters for AR and furniture shopping, and Spacely AI for professional-quality concept images.

The 6 Tools, Reviewed

1. Planner 5D — Best All-Rounder

Planner 5D combines AI-assisted design with genuine 3D planning: draw floor plans, furnish rooms from a 5,000+ item catalog, and walk through the result in first-person view. The AI "Smart Wizard" generates room layouts from a description or photo, and renders are realistic enough to plan real renovations around.

Free plan: Free tier with core 3D planning; premium (~$5–10/month) unlocks the full catalog and HD renders.

Strengths: Real floor-plan accuracy, huge furniture catalog, works on phone and desktop.

Best for: Homeowners planning actual renovations and renters testing layouts.

Weakness: Photo restyling is weaker than dedicated AI renderers; the interface takes an hour to learn.

2. RoomGPT — Best Free Instant Restyle

RoomGPT is the simplest tool on this list: upload a room photo, pick a style (modern, tropical, industrial, 40+ options), and get a redesigned render in under a minute. It's powered by generative AI rather than 3D modeling, which makes it fast and free — and perfect for brainstorming directions before committing.

Free plan: Several free generations per day; pro (~$9/month) for more renders and higher resolution.

Strengths: Dead simple, fast, and free to try.

Best for: Quick inspiration and seeing your room in styles you'd never imagine.

Weakness: Renders change furniture and proportions freely — don't treat output as a shopping list.

3. REimagineHome — Best Photorealistic Renders

REimagineHome produces the most convincing before-and-after images of the AI renderers: upload a photo and choose from 50+ design themes, exterior restyles, and landscaping. Results keep your room's structure while swapping finishes, furniture, and lighting convincingly — good enough for real-estate staging mockups.

Free plan: Limited free credits; paid from ~$29/month for regular use.

Strengths: Most realistic AI restyles; handles exteriors and landscaping too.

Best for: Visualizing a finished look and staging photos for property listings.

Weakness: Priciest option; credit-based pricing penalizes experimentation.

4. Homestyler — Best 3D Floor Plans

Autodesk's Homestyler is the serious planning tool: precise 2D/3D floor plans, real branded furniture models, and high-quality renders. It's less "AI magic photo" and more "professional design software with AI assistance" — the AI helps with auto-furnishing and lighting, but you stay in control of dimensions.

Free plan: Free basic tier; pro features from ~$10/month.

Strengths: Dimensional accuracy, real product models, strong render quality.

Best for: Detailed renovation planning where measurements matter.

Weakness: Steeper learning curve; photo restyling isn't its strength.

5. DecorMatters — Best AR and Shopping

DecorMatters turns your phone into a design studio: AR mode places true-to-scale furniture in your actual room through the camera, and a built-in marketplace lets you buy what you see. The AI recommends pieces based on your style quiz and existing decor, and a community feature shows real user designs.

Free plan: Free with ads; premium removes ads and unlocks exclusive items.

Strengths: See furniture in your real room before buying; buy-direct marketplace.

Best for: Furnishing a room and shopping with confidence.

Weakness: Product availability skews US/UK; AI styling suggestions are basic.

6. Spacely AI — Best Concept Images

Spacely AI (formerly ArchiVinci) generates concept-quality interior renders from photos or text prompts, with control over style, color palette, and mood. Interior designers use it for client presentations; homeowners use it to explore bold ideas cheaply. The "keep my furniture" mode restyles around what you already own.

Free plan: Free trial credits; paid from ~$10/month.

Strengths: Fine control over palette and mood; respects existing furniture on request.

Best for: Designers' concept boards and bold redesign exploration.

Weakness: Concept renders can suggest structurally impossible changes — verify before building.

Which One Should You Pick?

  • Just want to see possibilities: RoomGPT — free, instant, fun.
  • Planning a real renovation: Planner 5D or Homestyler — measurements you can build from.
  • Selling or staging a property: REimagineHome — the most convincing before-and-afters.
  • Buying furniture: DecorMatters — AR preview in your actual room.
  • Professional concept work: Spacely AI — palette and mood control for presentations.

Tips for Realistic Results

Three habits separate usable output from fantasy. First, photograph rooms in daylight from a corner — AI restyles clean, well-lit photos far better than dark cluttered ones. Second, combine tools: use RoomGPT for direction, then rebuild the winning look in Planner 5D with real dimensions and actual products. Third, treat every render as a starting sketch — AI happily suggests furniture that doesn't exist or walls that can't move. Verify sizes against your room before ordering anything.

FAQ

Can AI interior design replace a human designer?

For inspiration and early visualization, largely yes — and for free. A human designer still wins on building codes, structural feasibility, contractor coordination, and truly original taste. Use AI to arrive at the consultation with a clear vision, and you'll get more value from the professional.

Which AI interior design tool is free?

RoomGPT offers free daily renders, and Planner 5D, Homestyler, and DecorMatters all have usable free tiers. REimagineHome and Spacely AI give only limited trial credits — they're paid tools in practice.

Can these tools redesign my actual room from a photo?

Yes — RoomGPT, REimagineHome, and Spacely AI restyle uploaded photos directly. For planning that you can actually build from, Planner 5D and Homestyler are better: they work with measured floor plans rather than photos.

Do AI room renders show real furniture I can buy?

Mostly no — generative renders invent furniture. Exceptions: DecorMatters links renders to real products you can purchase, and Homestyler uses real branded models. Always verify a piece exists before falling in love with it.

Can I use AI designs for a rental property?

Absolutely — AI tools are ideal for renters, since renders let you test layouts without moving furniture. Focus on Planner 5D for space planning and DecorMatters' AR for checking that a sofa actually fits through the door.
