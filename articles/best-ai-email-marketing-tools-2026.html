7 Best AI Email Marketing Tools in 2026 (Automation, Subject Lines & Newsletters Compared)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read · Category: AI Marketing

Email is still the highest-ROI marketing channel most businesses have, and AI has changed how it's done. Today's platforms write your subject lines, pick the best send time per subscriber, predict who is about to churn, and draft campaigns from a one-sentence prompt. We compared seven popular tools on AI features, free plans, and who each suits.

Quick Verdict

Short version: Klaviyo for e-commerce AI, Mailchimp for beginner-friendly AI drafting, Brevo for budget all-in-one AI, ActiveCampaign for advanced AI automation, Kit for newsletter creators, MailerLite for the best value on small lists, and Omnisend for stores wanting AI-powered email plus SMS.

The 7 Tools, Reviewed

1. Klaviyo — Best for E-commerce AI

Klaviyo scores subscribers on predicted lifetime value, churn risk, and next order date, and builds segments from plain-language prompts ("people likely to buy in the next 30 days"). Product recommendations and subject lines pull from your catalog.

Free plan: Around 250 contacts and 500 sends/month. Paid from roughly $20–25/month.

Best for: Shopify and WooCommerce stores that want revenue-focused AI.

Weakness: Pricy as your list grows; overkill for simple newsletters.

2. Mailchimp — Best for Beginner-Friendly AI

An AI content optimizer drafts copy from a short prompt, generates subject lines with predicted performance, and recommends send times based on your audience's behavior. The creative assistant auto-builds branded designs, and AI A/B testing suggests what to test.

Free plan: Around 500 contacts with roughly 1,000 sends/month. Paid from roughly $13/month.

Best for: Small businesses sending their first campaigns with AI help.

Weakness: Pricing tiers rise steeply; AI analytics are lighter than Klaviyo's.

3. Brevo — Best Budget AI All-in-One

Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) bundles email, SMS, and WhatsApp with an AI copy assistant and send-time optimization per contact. Pricing is send-based, not contact-based — kind to big lists that email rarely.

Free plan: Unlimited contacts with around 300 emails/day. Paid from roughly $19/month.

Best for: Small businesses on a tight budget that still want AI copy help.

Weakness: AI automation and analytics are simpler than ActiveCampaign's.

4. ActiveCampaign — Best for Advanced AI Automation

The AI automation builder generates whole workflows from a text description ("re-engage customers who haven't bought in 60 days"), and predictive sending dispatches each email when that contact is most likely to open. Predictive lead scoring and churn prediction feed straight into the automations.

Free plan: No free plan — a 14-day trial only. Paid from roughly $29/month.

Best for: Marketers and agencies running complex AI automation.

Weakness: Steeper learning curve; beginners can feel lost.

5. Kit (formerly ConvertKit) — Best for Newsletter Creators

Kit's AI assistant drafts newsletter issues, subject lines, and landing-page copy in your voice, and smart recommendations grow everyone's list. Built-in Commerce lets you sell digital products and paid newsletters without a separate tool.

Free plan: Around 10,000 subscribers with limited features. Paid from roughly $29/month.

Best for: Creators and writers monetizing a newsletter audience.

Weakness: Weak on e-commerce predictions and SMS.

6. MailerLite — Best Value for Small Lists

A writing assistant drafts email content and subject lines, while Smart Sending delivers when each subscriber is most active, with auto-resends to non-openers. Combined with a very generous free tier, it's hard to beat on cost-per-email.

Free plan: Around 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 sends/month. Paid from roughly $10–13/month.

Best for: Bloggers, freelancers, and small businesses on a budget.

Weakness: AI segmentation is basic next to Klaviyo and ActiveCampaign.

7. Omnisend — Best E-commerce Email + SMS AI

Omnisend's AI writes subject lines and content from your product catalog, and pre-built workflows (abandoned cart, welcome series, post-purchase) come AI-tuned out of the box. Email, SMS, and push live in one dashboard — the friendliest plug-in-and-go store option.

Free plan: Around 250 contacts and 500 sends/month. Paid from roughly $16/month.

Best for: Small to mid-size stores wanting email plus SMS.

Weakness: Less flexible than ActiveCampaign for non-e-commerce workflows.

Which One Should You Pick?

  • E-commerce store: Klaviyo for deep AI predictions; Omnisend for email plus SMS at a lower price.
  • Newsletter creator: Kit — built for writers.
  • Small business on a budget: MailerLite or Brevo for the most AI help per dollar.
  • Agency or power marketer: ActiveCampaign — AI automation and predictive sending do real work for you.
  • Just starting out: Mailchimp — the AI content optimizer makes your first campaigns look professional.

FAQ

Is AI email marketing worth it?

For most senders, yes. AI subject lines and send-time optimization lift open rates, predictive segmentation protects deliverability, and drafting plus testing get much faster.

Which tool is best for a small list?

MailerLite's free tier (around 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 sends/month) is the most generous option, with AI drafting and Smart Sending included. Kit's free tier also allows around 10,000 subscribers with more limited features.

Do these tools replace copywriters?

Not fully. AI writes competent first drafts of subject lines, body copy, and newsletters, but the best campaigns still need a human eye for voice and brand. Treat AI as a fast junior copywriter that needs your review before anything goes out.

Which AI email tool is cheapest?

MailerLite has the lowest paid starting price (around $10–13/month) and the most generous free tier. Brevo is also strong value because pricing is send-based — good if you have a big list but email infrequently.

Can AI write whole campaigns?

Up to a point. ActiveCampaign and Klaviyo can generate complete workflows — emails, timing, segmentation — from a text description. Review the copy before sending, but AI now handles most of the heavy lifting.
