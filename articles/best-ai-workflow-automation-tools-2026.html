8 Best AI Workflow Automation Tools in 2026 (Zapier vs Make vs n8n Compared)

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read · Category: AI Productivity

Automation tools no longer just move data between apps — they run AI inside the workflow. The best build flows from a plain-English prompt and add AI steps (summarize, extract, classify, draft) anywhere. We compared eight: the giants (Zapier, Make), the self-hostable pair (n8n, Activepieces), and the AI-native wave (Lindy, Gumloop, Bardeen, Relay.app).

Quick Verdict

Short version: Zapier for the biggest app ecosystem, Make for the most value and a powerful builder, n8n for developers and self-hosting, Lindy for AI employees that handle whole roles, Gumloop for AI browser automation, Bardeen for browser scraping, Relay.app for AI workflows with human approvals, and Activepieces for the free open-source Zapier alternative.

The 8 Tools, Reviewed

1. Zapier — Best for App Ecosystem

The biggest name in automation: roughly 6,000 integrations, an AI Copilot that builds "Zaps" from a prompt, and AI steps that summarize, extract, or draft text.

Free plan: Around 100 tasks/month. Paid from roughly $20/month.

Best for: Teams wanting the biggest app library.

Weakness: Expensive at volume; AI feels bolted on.

2. Make — Best for Power Users on a Budget

Make's visual builder shows every step as a flowchart with routers, filters, and error handlers — far more flexible than Zapier's linear zaps. It connects 2,000+ apps, its AI assistant builds scenarios from a description, and AI agents can run them on their own.

Free plan: Around 1,000 operations/month. Paid from roughly $9/month.

Best for: Freelancers building complex flows on a budget.

Weakness: Steeper learning curve; fewer integrations.

3. n8n — Best for Developers and Self-Hosting

The developer favorite: fair-code licensed, free to self-host, and extendable with JavaScript or Python code nodes. AI agent nodes, chat triggers, and RAG workflows handle LLM pipelines the bigger names struggle with.

Free plan: Free self-hosted. Cloud from roughly $20/month.

Best for: Technical teams wanting control, privacy, and no per-run billing.

Weakness: You own hosting and fixes; overkill for non-technical users.

4. Lindy — Best AI Employees

Instead of building flows, you "hire" AI employees for roles like sales prospecting or meeting scheduling. Each Lindy learns your tools and SOPs, works your inbox, and asks a human before sensitive actions. Automation framed as staffing.

Free plan: Limited free trial. Paid from roughly $50/month.

Best for: Founders wanting outcomes ("book me meetings"), not wiring logic.

Weakness: Priced per AI employee; you see less of the underlying logic.

5. Gumloop — Best AI-Native Automation

Automation built AI-first: describe a workflow in plain language and it generates the pipeline. Its standout is AI browser automation — agents that navigate sites, fill forms, and scrape pages like a human.

Free plan: Free tier with limited monthly runs. Paid from roughly $37/month.

Best for: AI-powered research, scraping, and enrichment pipelines.

Weakness: Credit pricing can surprise heavy users; smaller integration library.

6. Bardeen — Best for Browser Automation and Spreadsheets

A Chrome extension with one-click "playbooks" that automate your open tabs: AI actions scrape sites into Google Sheets, draft emails, and summarize pages. The free plan is genuinely useful.

Free plan: Generous free playbook runs. Premium from roughly $15/month.

Best for: Prospecting, list building, and browser busywork.

Weakness: Limited backend scheduling; not a full cloud engine.

7. Relay.app — Best for AI-Assisted Team Workflows

Relay.app centers on AI steps plus human approvals: workflows summarize, extract, and draft with AI, then pause for a person to approve. One-click templates, 100+ integrations.

Free plan: Free tier available. Paid from roughly $29/month.

Best for: Teams wanting a human checkpoint before AI output ships.

Weakness: Smaller integration catalog than Zapier or Make.

8. Activepieces — Best Free Open-Source Alternative

The open-source answer to Zapier: MIT-licensed, free to self-host, 300+ pre-built "pieces", and an AI copilot that generates flows from descriptions.

Free plan: Free self-hosted, open source. Cloud from roughly $25/month.

Best for: Startups and privacy teams wanting Zapier-style flows for free.

Weakness: Smaller integration library; self-hosting needs some setup.

Which One Should You Pick?

  • Solo freelancer: Make's free tier (~1,000 ops/month) or Bardeen's free playbooks — both zero cost.
  • Small business: Zapier for reliability and app coverage; Make on a budget.
  • Developer or tech team: n8n self-hosted for full control, or Activepieces for a simpler free option.
  • Sales team: Lindy for AI employees running outreach, or Gumloop for AI scraping and enrichment.
  • Privacy-conscious self-hoster: n8n or Activepieces — both free to self-host; Activepieces is simpler, n8n more powerful.

FAQ

What is the best free automation tool?

Make's roughly 1,000 free operations a month is the most generous cloud option. Technical users get n8n and Activepieces free via self-hosting; Bardeen's free tier covers browser tasks.

Zapier vs Make — which is better?

Zapier wins on app coverage (~6,000 integrations) and polish; Make wins on price and power, with a more flexible builder and a bigger free tier. For most freelancers, Make is the better value.

Is n8n really free?

Yes, if you self-host. The fair-code license lets you run it on your own server with no per-run cost; the hosted cloud version starts at roughly $20/month.

Can AI build the automation for me?

Yes, mostly. Zapier's Copilot, Make's AI assistant, Gumloop, and Activepieces' copilot all generate working flows from prompts; Lindy skips building with ready-made AI employees. Review AI-built flows before they touch production data.

Which tool is best for AI agents?

n8n is best for custom AI agent pipelines (agent nodes, RAG, chat triggers). Gumloop is best for browser agents that click and scrape. Lindy is best for ready-made AI employees with no building.
