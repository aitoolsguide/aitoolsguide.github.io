What makes a good AI headshot

Judge any generator by these five things:

  • Realism: Does it keep your actual face — or a prettier stranger? Natural skin texture is the test; plastic-smooth skin is the classic giveaway.
  • Turnaround: Most tools deliver in 30–120 minutes. If an application is due tomorrow, speed matters.
  • Input photos: You need 10–20 varied selfies — different lighting, angles, expressions. The output is only as good as your uploads.
  • Professional suitability: Paid packages include full ownership for professional use.
  • Detail flaws: Zoom in on your final pick — warped ears and odd background blur are the usual failures.

1. Aragon AI

Aragon trains a personal model on your selfies and returns dozens of studio-style headshots in varied outfits and backgrounds. Realism is among the strongest here, especially for corporate-style shots. Turnaround is typically 60–90 minutes; paid packages include full ownership — check current pricing. Drawback: with too-similar selfies, results can drift from your real face — input variety matters.

2. HeadshotPro

HeadshotPro targets professionals and teams with consistently framed corporate headshots — office backgrounds, business attire, studio-like lighting. Very good realism for straightforward business portraits: the "safe" pick for LinkedIn. Turnaround is usually under two hours; team packages cover whole departments without a studio booking. Paid packages include full ownership — check current pricing. Drawback: the style range skews corporate and can feel repetitive.

3. Secta AI

Secta AI is built around speed — one of the fastest in the category, returning headshots in as little as 10–30 minutes on many plans. Realism is solid given the speed; skin texture and facial features generally hold up at profile-photo size. Paid packages include ownership for professional use — check current pricing. Drawback: faster options offer fewer refinement controls, so fewer shots make the final cut.

4. BetterPic

BetterPic adds human review: after the AI generates your headshots, human editors refine details on some plans — directly attacking artifacts like warped ears and strange backgrounds. Realism on the finished picks is excellent. Turnaround is slower, up to a few hours. It suits high-stakes uses like executive profiles; paid packages cover professional use — check current pricing. Drawback: among the pricier options — overkill if you just need one LinkedIn photo.

5. ProPhotos

ProPhotos is the straightforward budget option: upload your selfies, get a batch of clean business portraits back in about 30–60 minutes. Realism is good rather than top-tier — fine at profile-photo size, though some shots show telltale over-smoothed skin. Paid packages grant full rights — check current pricing. One honest drawback: style variety is limited, so you may get a gallery of very similar-looking shots.

6. StudioShot

StudioShot asks for a larger set of input selfies than most competitors and uses them to build a more accurate model of your face, then renders portraits with natural-looking studio lighting — quality over speed. Realism is among the best here: faces stay faithful to the input. Turnaround is slower, often 2–3 hours. Paid packages include full ownership — check current pricing. Drawback: needing 20+ selfies and 2–3 hours makes it the least convenient for urgent needs.

7. TryItOnAI

TryItOnAI is the most affordable entry here — the same basic loop (upload selfies, get studio-style portraits back) with a simpler presentation. Realism is decent for the price, but likeness consistency is the weakest of the seven: some shots will look like you, others clearly won't, so budget time to sift for the best ones. Turnaround is typically under an hour. Paid packages cover professional use — check current pricing. Drawback: the lower keeper rate costs you more time per great photo.

Which one should you pick?

  • LinkedIn profile: Aragon AI or HeadshotPro.
  • Team photos at scale: HeadshotPro — matching styles, no photographer.
  • Budget: TryItOnAI or ProPhotos — less consistent, far cheaper.
  • Fastest turnaround: Secta AI.
  • Most realistic: StudioShot or BetterPic.

Universal rule: upload varied, well-lit selfies — different angles, expressions, and backgrounds. A model trained on ten near-identical bathroom selfies produces a near-identical-looking stranger.

FAQ

Do AI headshots look real?

The best ones, yes — at profile-photo sizes, top tools produce portraits most people would not question. At full zoom you can still spot giveaways: plastic-smooth skin, warped ears, odd earrings. Always inspect your chosen photo at full size first.

Can employers or recruiters tell?

Sometimes. Recruiters recognize over-smoothed "AI skin". A natural-looking headshot that keeps your real face shape rarely raises suspicion. Pick the most natural shot, not the most flattering one.

Are AI headshots acceptable for LinkedIn and job applications?

Generally, yes. LinkedIn has no rule against them, and paid packages give you full ownership. One caveat: if the photo drifts from your real appearance, it can backfire at an in-person interview.

How many photos do I need to upload?

Most tools ask for 10–20 selfies; StudioShot wants 20+. Use natural lighting, varied angles and expressions — no sunglasses, hats, or group shots. Never upload near-identical photos; variety teaches the model your real face.

Are AI headshots cheaper than a studio photographer?

By a wide margin. A professional session typically costs $100–$300+ for a handful of retouched photos, while AI packages cost a fraction of that for dozens of portraits. The trade-off is control: a photographer coaches your poses in person; AI gives you volume and convenience.

Can I use AI headshots for a whole team?

Yes — one of the strongest use cases. Team packages (notably HeadshotPro) give every employee matching-style headshots without a studio booking. Have each person approve their own photos before they go live.
