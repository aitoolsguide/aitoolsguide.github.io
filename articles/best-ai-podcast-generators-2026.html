7 Best AI Podcast Generators in 2026 (Start Your Show Today)

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read · Category: AI Creativity

Podcasting used to need a microphone, a quiet room, and hours of editing. Now AI can generate voices, write scripts, edit audio by editing text, and publish episodes with a few clicks. Seven tools compared — NotebookLM, Podcastle, Descript, Wondercraft, Play.ht, Listnr and ElevenLabs — covering everything from AI-hosted shows to professional editing.

What to look for in an AI podcast tool

  • Voice quality: Robotic voices kill retention — listen to samples before committing.
  • Editing workflow: Text-based editing (delete words to delete audio) saves hours.
  • Publishing: Does it distribute to Spotify and Apple Podcasts, or do you handle RSS yourself?
  • Voice cloning consent: Any tool that clones voices needs safeguards — check them.

1. NotebookLM

Google's research tool became famous for its Audio Overviews: upload documents, articles, or links and it generates a natural two-host conversation summarizing them — complete with banter and follow-up questions. The voices are remarkably lifelike, and it's free within generous limits. Drawback: you can't fully script what the hosts say, customization is limited to guidance prompts, and it's not a publishing platform — you export audio and handle distribution yourself. Best for turning research into listenable audio fast.

2. Podcastle

The all-in-one AI podcast studio: record (video and audio), edit in a text-based editor, enhance with AI audio cleanup, generate voiceovers from text, and publish from one dashboard. Plans start free with watermarked exports; paid plans from around $12-25 per month unlock full features. Drawback: advanced editing lags dedicated tools like Descript, and the free tier's limits bite quickly. The fastest path from idea to published episode here.

3. Descript

The professional pick for editing: it transcribes your audio into text, and you edit by deleting words — the audio follows. Overdub creates a synthetic clone of your voice for corrections, filler-word removal is automatic, and Studio Sound polishes recordings. Free plan includes limited transcription; paid plans from around $12-24 per month. Drawback: AI voice generation is secondary to editing — it's for real recorded podcasts, not fully synthetic ones. If you record with your own voice, nothing edits faster.

4. Wondercraft

Built for fully AI-generated shows: type a script, pick from hyper-realistic voices in multiple languages, add music and sound effects, and publish — episodes can be generated in minutes. It also offers AI hosts and multi-speaker layouts. Plans start around $29 per month (higher than most here). Drawback: the price, and fully synthetic shows still feel synthetic over long episodes. Best for branded content, internal comms, and rapid multi-language versions of a show.

5. Play.ht

A voice-generation specialist with podcast features: hundreds of lifelike voices across 100+ languages, podcast hosting with RSS distribution, and an API for automated shows. Free tier includes limited generation; paid plans from around $19 per month. Drawback: editing and production features are thin — pair it with an editor for polished shows. Strong pick for multi-language podcasts and turning blogs into audio feeds.

6. Listnr

The budget synthetic-podcast option: text-to-speech with 1000+ voices in 140+ languages, podcast hosting, RSS feeds, and distribution to major platforms included. Free plan allows limited publishing; paid plans from around $7-19 per month make it one of the cheapest full pipelines here. Drawback: voice quality trails Wondercraft and ElevenLabs, and editing is minimal. Ideal for testing a fully AI show cheaply.

7. ElevenLabs

The voice-quality leader: its AI voices are the hardest to distinguish from human speech, with excellent emotional range and 30+ languages. Its Studio product turns scripts and source material into long-form audio with multiple speakers. Free tier is generous for testing; paid plans from around $5-99 per month depending on character quotas. Drawback: it's a voice engine, not a podcast platform — you'll handle hosting and distribution separately. Use it when voice quality is the whole point.

Which one should you pick?

  • You want a show from your research today: NotebookLM — free and genuinely impressive.
  • You record with your own voice: Descript for editing, Podcastle if you want recording through publishing in one place.
  • Fully synthetic show: Wondercraft for quality, Listnr for budget, Play.ht for many languages.
  • Voice quality above all: ElevenLabs — then host wherever you like.
  • Repurposing content: Play.ht or ElevenLabs to turn blogs and newsletters into audio feeds.
  • Best free option: NotebookLM — nothing else free sounds this natural.

One caution for all of them: disclose AI involvement. Platforms increasingly require it, and listeners trust shows that are upfront about how they're made. Fully synthetic shows also need human fact-checking — AI voices confidently narrate AI hallucinations.

FAQ

Can AI really host a podcast?

Yes — NotebookLM's two-host format fooled plenty of listeners. Long-form fully synthetic shows still feel less spontaneous than human hosts, but for explainers and summaries they work well.

Do AI podcasts get monetized?

On YouTube and Spotify, disclosure rules apply and reused/low-effort content policies can limit monetization. Original scripts with genuine production effort fare best.

Which tool is best for beginners?

Podcastle — record, edit, enhance, and publish without leaving the app. NotebookLM is the easiest start if you only want AI-generated audio.

Can I clone my own voice for a podcast?

Yes — Descript's Overdub, ElevenLabs, and Play.ht all offer voice cloning. Legitimate tools require you to verify the voice is yours; never clone someone else's without consent.

How do I distribute an AI podcast to Spotify and Apple?

Use a tool with built-in hosting and RSS (Podcastle, Wondercraft, Play.ht, Listnr), or generate audio elsewhere and upload to a podcast host like Spotify for Creators, Buzzsprout, or Transistor.

What does an AI podcast cost?

Free to start: NotebookLM and free tiers cover experimentation. A serious fully-AI show runs $12-30 per month; a voice-first show on ElevenLabs can start around $5 per month plus hosting.
