Best AI Image Generators Compared: Midjourney vs DALL-E vs Flux

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read · Category: Comparisons

Three image generators dominate the conversation: Midjourney's artistic flair, DALL-E's ease of use, and Flux's open-source flexibility. We compared them on the things creators actually care about.

Midjourney

The artist's choice. Midjourney produces the most beautiful, stylized images - perfect for cartoon channels, thumbnails and creative projects. Its character-reference feature is unmatched for keeping the same character consistent across dozens of images, which is critical for series content. Downsides: no free tier, and the interface confuses beginners.

DALL-E

The pragmatic choice. Built into ChatGPT, so you describe what you want conversationally and iterate fast. Best of the three at rendering readable text inside images (great for thumbnails with titles). Image quality is clean but less "wow" than Midjourney for artistic styles.

Flux

The flexible choice. Open-source roots mean free and self-hosted options exist, and the realism is outstanding - often mistaken for photography. The ecosystem of community tools and fine-tunes is huge. Slightly more setup, but the price-to-quality ratio is unbeatable.

Which Should You Choose?

  • Cartoon/animated content: Midjourney - character consistency wins.
  • Thumbnails with text: DALL-E - readable text, fast iteration.
  • Budget / realism: Flux - free options with stunning quality.
  • Already paying for ChatGPT Plus: just use DALL-E, it is included.
Creator combo: many successful channels use Midjourney for characters and DALL-E for quick thumbnail variations. You do not have to marry one tool.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI image generator is free?
Flux has free options through various platforms and can be run locally. Midjourney has no free tier; DALL-E requires a ChatGPT Plus subscription for full access.
Which is best for YouTube thumbnails?
DALL-E for text-heavy thumbnails, Midjourney for character-driven artistic ones. Both work - pick based on your thumbnail style.
Can I use AI images commercially?
Generally yes on paid plans (check each service's current terms). Free tiers may have restrictions - always verify before commercial use.
How do I keep characters consistent?
Use Midjourney's character reference feature, or generate a base character image and use image-to-image / inpainting in any tool for new poses and scenes.
