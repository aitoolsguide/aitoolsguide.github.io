7 Best AI Tools for Real Estate Agents in 2026 (Listings, Leads & Virtual Staging)

Updated October 2026 · 7 min read · Category: Business

A listing lives or dies on its first impression: the photos, the description, and how fast someone follows up on the enquiry. AI tools now handle all three — writing listing copy, staging empty rooms virtually for a fraction of a stager's fee, and answering leads at midnight. Here are the seven best AI tools for real estate agents in 2026.

Quick Verdict

Short version: Matterport for 3D tours buyers trust, BoxBrownie for fast photo editing and virtual staging, ListingAI for listing descriptions in seconds, Structurely for answering leads instantly, Follow Up Boss for keeping every lead organized, REimagineHome for photorealistic restyles, and Lofty for an all-in-one AI-powered CRM and marketing platform.

The 7 Tools, Reviewed

1. Matterport — Best 3D Virtual Tours

Matterport turns a phone scan into a walkable 3D digital twin of a property. Buyers explore the home room by room from anywhere in the world, and listings with a Matterport tour consistently attract more views and more qualified enquiries than photo-only listings.

Free plan: One free active space; paid plans from ~$10/month for more spaces and features.

Strengths: Trusted 3D standard, automatic floor plans, works from a phone.

Best for: Agents selling remotely or in competitive markets where the tour decides the viewing.

Weakness: Large or cluttered homes take longer to scan well; quality depends on the capture.

2. BoxBrownie — Best Photo Editing and Virtual Staging

BoxBrownie is the workhorse behind many agents' listing photos: AI-enhanced photo editing, virtual staging of empty rooms, floor-plan redraws, and twilight conversions, all delivered within about 24 hours.

Free plan: Pay-per-image pricing (editing from ~$1.60, staging from ~$24 per image); no subscription required.

Strengths: Fast turnaround, pay-as-you-go, human-checked results.

Best for: Agents who want professional listing photos without a photographer on every property.

Weakness: Staged images must be labeled as virtually staged in many markets — check local disclosure rules.

3. ListingAI — Best Listing Descriptions

ListingAI writes property descriptions from your basic inputs — beds, baths, features, neighborhood — in seconds, in a tone you choose. It removes the blank-page problem for agents listing several homes a week,

Free plan: Free trial; paid plans from ~$19/month for regular listing volume.

Strengths: Fast, tone-controlled copy, built for property language.

Best for: High-volume agents and teams listing multiple properties weekly.

Weakness: Always verify facts — AI copy can invent features that are not in the home.

4. Structurely — Best Instant Lead Response

Structurely's AI assistant answers new leads by text and email within seconds, qualifies them with natural conversation, and books viewings straight into your calendar. Speed matters enormously in real estate — the first agent to respond usually wins the conversation.

Free plan: No free tier; pricing from ~$199/month per agent, typically bundled with lead sources.

Strengths: Instant response, natural qualification conversations, automatic booking.

Best for: Agents buying online leads or running ads that generate after-hours enquiries.

Weakness: The price only makes sense if you have real lead volume to capture.

5. Follow Up Boss — Best Lead Management CRM

Follow Up Boss is the CRM built for how agents actually work: leads from every portal flow into one inbox, AI-assisted action plans remind you who to call and when, and smart lists surface the hottest leads first.

Free plan: No free tier; plans from ~$69/month, with team and brokerage tiers above that.

Strengths: Lead-source integrations, action plans, team-friendly.

Best for: Agents and teams juggling leads from multiple portals and ad campaigns.

Weakness: Needs setup and discipline — a CRM only works if you actually work it daily.

6. REimagineHome — Best Photorealistic Restyles

REimagineHome restyles listing photos with AI: empty rooms get furnished, dated rooms get modernized, and exteriors get a fresh look, all in photorealistic renders. It works especially well for renovations and fixer-uppers.

Free plan: Limited free credits; paid plans from ~$29/month.

Strengths: Most realistic AI restyles, handles exteriors and landscaping, fast.

Best for: Listings that need imagination — empty homes, dated interiors, renovation projects.

Weakness: Credit-based pricing penalizes experimentation; disclose AI-enhanced images where required.

7. Lofty — Best All-in-One AI Platform

Lofty (formerly Chime) bundles a CRM, IDX website, AI-powered lead generation, and automated marketing into one platform. For agents or brokerages that want one system instead of five subscriptions, Lofty is the most complete option.

Free plan: No free tier; pricing varies by team size and features, typically from a few hundred dollars monthly.

Strengths: Everything in one place, AI lead scoring, built-in websites and ads.

Best for: Teams and brokerages consolidating tools into a single platform.

Weakness: Higher cost and a bigger setup effort than single-purpose tools.

Which One Should You Pick?

Starting out on a budget? Matterport's free space, BoxBrownie's pay-per-image model, and ListingAI give you a professional listing for very little money. Losing leads to slow follow-up? Structurely answers instantly, and Follow Up Boss keeps every lead organized afterwards. Selling empty or dated homes? BoxBrownie staging or REimagineHome restyles make the biggest visual difference. Running a team? Lofty replaces several tools with one platform, and Follow Up Boss is the CRM most teams standardize on.

Tips for AI Listings That Still Feel Human

Verify every fact in AI copy. Descriptions that invent a renovated kitchen create legal trouble — read every description before it publishes. Disclose virtual staging. Many markets require staged photos to be labeled; the label costs nothing and protects you. Keep your voice in the copy. Use AI for the first draft, then edit in the local details only you know. Respond fast, then be human. AI should win you the first reply; the relationship that closes the deal is yours.

FAQ

Do virtually staged photos really help sell homes?

Yes — staged listings consistently attract more views and enquiries than empty rooms, because buyers struggle to judge scale and potential in an unfurnished space. Just disclose that images are virtually staged where your market requires it.

Can AI write accurate listing descriptions?

AI writes fluent, persuasive copy, but it can invent features. Treat it as a first draft and verify every claim against the actual property before publishing.

Is it legal to use AI-enhanced listing photos?

Generally yes, but rules vary: many MLS boards and regulators require disclosure of virtually staged or materially altered images, and photos must never misrepresent the property's condition or surroundings.

Which AI tool is best for a new agent on a budget?

Matterport's free tier for 3D tours, BoxBrownie's pay-per-image editing, and ListingAI for copy give a new agent a professional listing kit for under $30 a month.
