7 Best AI Tools for Restaurants in 2026 (Orders, Reservations & Less Waste)

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read · Category: Business

Running a restaurant means answering phones during the dinner rush, filling tables on a slow Tuesday, and throwing away food you paid for. AI tools now help with all three — and the best ones pay for themselves by capturing orders and bookings a busy team would otherwise miss. Here are seven tools worth a serious look, from point-of-sale systems to an AI that answers your phone.

What to look for in an AI restaurant tool

  • Where it saves money: Captured orders, filled tables and less waste beat a pretty dashboard every time.
  • Fit with your setup: A tool that fights your existing POS or reservation book will cost more time than it saves.
  • Guest experience: AI should make ordering and booking easier for customers, not feel like a barrier.
  • Real costs: Watch for per-order fees, per-location pricing and hardware costs on top of the subscription.

1. Toast

Toast is the all-in-one starting point for many restaurants: point of sale, online ordering, payments, payroll and reporting in one system, with AI features that surface sales trends, suggest menu pricing changes and flag your busiest hours. Because ordering, kitchen display and payments all live in one place, staff training is simpler and you get a single view of the business. Drawback: it is a full ecosystem, so switching away later is painful, and hardware plus payment-processing costs add up — check current pricing for your country and size.

2. SevenRooms

SevenRooms is built for restaurants that live on reservations: it manages bookings, waitlists and guest profiles, and uses that history to seat regulars well, spot VIPs and send targeted offers to guests who have not visited in a while. Its marketing tools let you fill slow nights with the right offer instead of a blanket discount. Drawback: it shines brightest for full-service restaurants; a fast takeaway spot will use only a fraction of it. Pricing is typically quoted per location — ask for a demo price.

3. Popmenu

Popmenu focuses on the menu and the marketing around it: interactive menus, a website, online ordering and automated email and text campaigns that promote dishes, events and slow-night specials. Its AI helps write and target those campaigns, so a small team can run marketing that used to need an agency. Drawback: if you already have a strong website and ordering setup, you may be paying twice for the same pieces. Plans are usually monthly per location — check current pricing.

4. Owner.com

Owner.com helps restaurants take more direct orders instead of losing a slice of every sale to delivery marketplaces. It builds an ordering website and app for your restaurant, runs AI-assisted marketing to bring guests back, and aims to turn one-time marketplace customers into repeat direct customers. For restaurants paying heavy commission fees, even a modest shift to direct ordering is a big win. Drawback: results depend on your existing customer base and marketing effort — it is a tool, not a magic tap of new diners. Pricing is typically a monthly fee plus a small per-order charge.

5. Slang.ai

Slang.ai answers your restaurant's phone with an AI voice assistant, 24 hours a day: it takes reservations, answers common questions about hours, location and dietary options, and routes complex calls to your team. Restaurants miss a surprising share of calls during service — every missed call can be a lost table — so this one directly protects revenue. Drawback: callers who want a human may be frustrated, so keep a clear path to a real person and test the voice yourself before launch. Pricing is usually per location per month.

6. Kea

Kea is another AI phone specialist, focused on taking accurate phone orders: it handles ordering, upsells add-ons politely, and sends the order straight to your POS or kitchen system. For restaurants where the phone never stops during peak hours, it frees staff to cook and serve instead of taking orders. Drawback: accuracy depends on a well-structured menu and clear item names — messy menus produce messy orders. Pricing is typically quoted per location.

7. Winnow

Winnow tackles the cost most restaurants ignore: food waste. A smart scale and camera above the bin identify what is being thrown away, and the software reports which dishes and prep items are costing you most. Kitchens that act on the data routinely cut waste — and food costs — by a meaningful margin. Drawback: it needs staff buy-in to log waste consistently, and the hardware makes it a bigger upfront commitment than a software-only tool. Pricing is quoted per site.

Which one should you pick?

  • Starting or replacing your POS: Toast — one system for ordering, payments and reporting.
  • Full-service restaurant: SevenRooms for reservations and repeat-guest marketing.
  • Losing money to delivery apps: Owner.com to grow direct ordering.
  • Missing calls during service: Slang.ai for reservations and questions, Kea if phone orders dominate.
  • Food costs creeping up: Winnow — waste data usually pays for itself fastest.
  • Small team, weak marketing: Popmenu for menus, website and automated campaigns.

Start with the leak that costs you most — missed calls, marketplace commission or wasted food — and fix that first. One tool that solves a real pain beats three tools that look impressive in a demo.

FAQ

Will customers accept an AI answering the phone?

Most do, as long as it is fast, accurate and offers a human when asked. Test it as a customer would — call your own restaurant — before going live.

Do I need to replace my POS to use AI tools?

Not always. Tools like Slang.ai, Kea and SevenRooms integrate with many popular POS systems, but check compatibility with yours before signing up.

How much can AI reduce food waste?

Results vary by kitchen, but restaurants using waste-tracking tools commonly report cutting waste by 30–50% once they act on the data. The savings come from smaller prep batches and menu tweaks, not from the tool alone.

Is AI ordering worth it for a small restaurant?

If you pay high marketplace commissions or miss phone orders during rushes, yes — even a small shift to direct or AI-captured orders adds up. If most orders already come in smoothly, fix marketing or waste first.

Do these tools work outside the US?

Availability varies by country and by tool — Toast, SevenRooms and Popmenu are strongest in the US, while others have wider coverage. Confirm availability, language support and payment processing for your country before committing.
