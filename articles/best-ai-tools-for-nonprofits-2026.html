7 Best AI Tools for Nonprofits in 2026 (Fundraising, Donors & Outreach)

Updated November 2026 · 9 min read · Category: Business

Nonprofits run on small teams and big missions: every hour spent on admin is an hour not spent on the cause. AI tools now help with the jobs that drain nonprofit staff most — writing fundraising appeals, finding likely major donors, and turning one-time gifts into repeat donations. Here are seven tools worth a serious look, from free fundraising platforms to donor intelligence built for small teams.

What to look for in an AI nonprofit tool

  • Fees that respect donations: Platform fees and payment-processing charges come straight out of gifts. Compare the true cost per donation, not just the headline price.
  • Donor relationships, not just transactions: The best tools track giving history, segment supporters, and automate thank-yous and follow-ups.
  • Small-team usability: If it needs a specialist to run, a small nonprofit will not use it. Favor tools your current team can learn in a day.
  • Compliance and trust: Donor data is sensitive. Check data security, receipt handling, and tax-receipt support for your country.

1. Givebutter

Givebutter is the friendly starting point for small and mid-sized nonprofits: donation forms, fundraising pages, peer-to-peer campaigns, events, and auctions, with AI-assisted drafting for campaign pages and appeals. Its free plan — funded by optional donor tips — makes it genuinely accessible to organizations with no software budget, and its pages are built to share well on social media. Drawback: advanced donor management and reporting are lighter than a full CRM, so larger organizations may outgrow it. Check current platform and processing fees before launch.

2. Classy

Classy, part of GoFundMe, is built for campaigns that need to look great and convert: polished fundraising pages, peer-to-peer fundraising, recurring giving, and events, with AI tools that help write and optimize campaign copy. Nonprofits use it for galas, giving days, and year-end campaigns where presentation directly affects results. Drawback: it is priced for organizations with real fundraising volume, and the full feature set takes time to learn. Pricing is typically an annual subscription plus processing fees — request a quote.

3. Virtuous

Virtuous is a donor CRM designed around responsiveness: it tracks every donor interaction, scores donor engagement, and uses automation to suggest the next best action — a thank-you call, a personalized email, or an upgrade ask — at the right moment. Its AI features help segment supporters and personalize outreach at a scale a small team could never do by hand. Drawback: it is a full CRM, so setup and data migration take effort, and it suits organizations ready to invest in donor management. Pricing is quoted per organization.

4. Keela

Keela is a nonprofit CRM built for small teams: donor management, email campaigns, donation tracking, and reporting in one affordable package, with AI-assisted writing for appeals and automated workflows for thank-yous and renewals. It is popular with grassroots organizations because it covers the essentials without enterprise complexity or pricing. Drawback: its fundraising-page and event tools are more basic than Givebutter or Classy, so some organizations pair it with a separate campaign tool. Pricing is usually monthly, scaled to contacts.

5. DonorSearch

DonorSearch answers a specific, high-value question: who among your supporters could give more? It screens your donor list against wealth, philanthropic, and giving-history data, and its AI scoring highlights the most promising major-gift prospects so your team focuses outreach where it counts. Drawback: it is a research tool, not a CRM or fundraising platform — you still need somewhere to run the campaign. Pricing is typically per screening or subscription.

6. Funraise

Funraise combines fundraising pages, peer-to-peer campaigns, and donor management with strong analytics: it tracks which campaigns, emails, and segments actually convert, and its AI features help personalize donor journeys and optimize ask amounts. Drawback: pricing is geared to organizations with steady fundraising volume, and smaller nonprofits may find Givebutter a better starting point. Pricing is quoted per organization.

7. Canva

Canva is not a fundraising platform, but it is the tool most nonprofits actually open every day: social graphics, appeal letters, event flyers, annual-report layouts, and short videos, with AI features that generate images, draft copy, and resize designs for every channel. Nonprofits get a discounted or free Pro plan in many countries through Canva for Nonprofits, which makes it the cheapest way to look professional. Drawback: it will not collect donations or manage donors — pair it with Givebutter, Classy, or Keela. Check eligibility for the nonprofit plan in your country.

Which one should you pick?

  • Just starting, tight budget: Givebutter for fundraising plus Canva for design — both nearly free.
  • Big campaign or giving day: Classy for pages that convert, or Funraise if you want campaign analytics.
  • Donors slipping through the cracks: Keela for small teams, Virtuous if you are ready to invest in donor management.
  • Planning a major-gift push: DonorSearch first — know who to call before you write the appeal.
  • Outreach that looks amateur: Canva for Nonprofits — the fastest visible upgrade for most organizations.

Start with the leak that costs you most — donations lost to clunky pages, donors never thanked properly, or major prospects never identified — and fix that first. A tool that raises real money beats a dashboard nobody opens.

FAQ

Are free fundraising platforms really free?

Mostly. Platforms like Givebutter offer free plans funded by optional donor tips, but payment-processing fees still apply to every donation. Read the fee breakdown before promising your board a cost.

Do small nonprofits need a CRM?

Once you pass a few hundred donors, yes — a spreadsheet stops being reliable for thank-yous, renewals, and segmentation. Keela is a good first CRM for small teams.

Is donor prospect research ethical?

When done transparently and with publicly available data, yes — it is standard practice in fundraising. Use it to prioritize respectful outreach, not to pressure donors, and follow your country's privacy rules.

Can AI write our fundraising appeals?

AI can draft strong first versions and personalize them at scale, but the final appeal should carry a real story from your organization, reviewed by a human. Donors can tell when a message has no heart in it.

How do nonprofits get Canva Pro for free?

Through Canva for Nonprofits, which offers free Pro access to eligible registered nonprofits in many countries. Apply with your registration documents.
